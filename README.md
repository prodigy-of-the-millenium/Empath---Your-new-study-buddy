# Empath---Your-new-study-buddy

There are many study apps in the market, but none of them focus on your mental health. That is our motto—our USP. The best way to increase efficiency is to ensure excellent health, as a healthy mind resides in a healthy body. Our goal is to help you study effectively. <br> 

As a student myself, I know how hard it is to avoid distractions in this age of short-form content. To prevent distraction, we aim to be the one-stop, be-all platform so that you never have to switch to YouTube, Instagram, or any other social media to take a break—making sure you stay focused. <br> 

We aim to be a one-stop platform so you never have to leave your study environment.
🔍 Smart Emotion Detection <br> 
We use integrated APIs to: <br> 
Analyze your face <br> 
Detect your emotions <br>

If you’re feeling low, stressed, or just off, the app takes you to a wellness corner. <br> 
In the wellness corner, you can:
🎵 Lo-fi music <br>
👀 20-20-20 rule <br>
🌬️ 4-7-8 breathing <br>
🧘 Guided meditation <br>
🎧 Brown noise <br>
😂 Fun content (like cat videos) <br>
🎮 Mini games <br>
🤖 In-built chatbot <br>

Once you feel better, you are guided back to Study Mode. <br> 
📚 Study Modes <br> 

We provide four different modes tailored to your mindset: <br> 
⚔️ Revenge Mode - Beat your rival <br> 
😴 Lazy Mode - Start slow when exams are far away <br> 
😊 Normal / Happy Mode - Balanced studying <br> 
😰 Stress Mode - High-focus, urgency-driven
